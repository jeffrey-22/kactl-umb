Sum of first $k$ row lengths of Young Tableau = max size of $k$ disjoint weakly increasing subseq = 
length of longest subseq whose longest strictly decreasing subseq has length $\leq$ k. 

First $k$ cols = $k$ disjoint strictly decreasing subseq =
subseq whose longest weakly increasing subseq has length $\leq$ k.

Hook-Length formula: \# of Young Tableaus with same shape is $\frac{n!}{\Pi h(x,y)}$ 
where $h(x,y)$ is the \# of squares directly below or to the right of square $(x,y)$.
